Hi! I'm Songqi, also known as Carson. I am currently an Algorithm Engineer at XGRIDS, working on 3D vision and LiDAR point cloud algorithms. I received my master's degree from the Ocean University of China, where I was advised by Prof. Cai Qing at VMV Lab.

你好，我是孔松琦。目前在 XGRIDS 担任算法工程师，从事三维视觉与激光雷达点云算法研发。硕士毕业于中国海洋大学，师从蔡青教授，就读于 VMV Lab。

My interests lie in 3D vision, LiDAR and point cloud algorithms in autonomous driving, and 3D reconstruction from uncalibrated scenarios. Outside of research I enjoy building small tools with vibe coding, see Vibing for what I have made.

研究课题包括三维视觉、自动驾驶场景中的激光雷达与点云算法，以及非标定场景下的三维重建。研究之外喜欢用 Vibe Coding 做点小工具，见 Vibing。
